The opportunity

A $200 billion market priced in the dark.

When a pension plan sells $400 million of seasoned fund interests, the price arrives as a single number — a percentage of net asset value — negotiated privately among a short list of specialist buyers. Two funds of the same vintage can clear 38 points apart. There is no exchange, no screen, no published benchmark for what LP interests trade at. Pricing opacity is the market's defining feature — and its biggest inefficiency.

SecondariesPricing.com names the missing dataset. It is the natural home for the industry's first secondaries price tape — discount-to-NAV benchmarks by vintage and strategy, GP-led pricing data, and the spread analysis every allocator, advisor, and secondaries buyer wants and no one publishes.
